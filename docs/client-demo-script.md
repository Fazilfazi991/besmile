# BSmile CRM client demo script (15–20 minutes)

Use only fictional `DEMO-` records in a clean demo workspace. Do not display QA accounts, personal bank details, production secrets, or real client data.

1. **Super Admin overview (2 min):** Sign in, show dashboard, employee directory, and the Roles & Access page. Explain role authority and time-bound direct grants.
2. **Attendance and leave (2 min):** Show the employee attendance card/calendar, then submit/view a leave request and its approval status.
3. **Tasks (2 min):** Show task assignment, My Tasks, an employee status update, and the scoped task-management view.
4. **Communication (2 min):** Show a targeted announcement, in-app notification, and a simple internal chat attachment using a non-sensitive file.
5. **CRM (3 min):** Open a demo lead, show compact lead details, follow-up number and due state, then convert to a sale in INR.
6. **Finance (3 min):** Show a demo income/expense transaction, private receipt preview, invoice creation/payment status, and payroll payment linkage.
7. **Reports and employee experience (2 min):** Show a report, then switch to the employee dashboard/profile to demonstrate the end-to-end workflow.

Close by explaining the audit trail, RLS isolation, and the post-launch support/smoke-test process. Leave 2–4 minutes for questions.
